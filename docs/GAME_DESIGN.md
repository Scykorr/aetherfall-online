# Game design baseline

## Pillars

1. Readable 3/4 combat.
2. Small, dense zones rather than empty giant maps.
3. Rare drops can change build behavior.
4. Social play is valuable but solo play remains viable.
5. Item progression has risk/resource sinks without routine destruction of prized gear.
6. Original world, silhouettes, names and mechanics expression.

## Original differentiator: Echo Imprints

Rare creature drops called Echo Imprints modify skill behavior rather than merely adding flat stats.

Example:
- thrust leaves a short rift trail;
- heal also creates a small shield;
- dash loses damage but gains another charge.

This is a project concept, not a copy of Royal Quest's card implementation.
